If you drove at home, you will want to know whether you can keep driving in Ireland. The answer depends on **where your licence was issued**. Driving licences are handled by the National Driver Licence Service (NDLS).

## Three groups of licences

**1. EU and EEA licences**
You can drive in Ireland on a valid EU or EEA licence until it expires. You can exchange it for an Irish licence if you want, and you must do so within 10 years of it expiring.

**2. Licences from "recognised states"**
Ireland has exchange agreements with certain countries and states outside the EU, called recognised states. They include the UK, and the list also includes places such as Australia, Japan, South Africa, South Korea and Switzerland. Once you take up normal residence in Ireland, you must either exchange the licence or apply for a new Irish one. Check the current NDLS list, as it changes.

**3. Licences from other countries**
If your country has no exchange agreement with Ireland, you can't swap your licence. You will need to go through the Irish licensing process, usually a learner permit and a driving test. Holders of foreign licences that can't be exchanged may be eligible for a **reduced** Essential Driver Training programme.

## What you need to exchange

- a **Public Services Card** and a verified **MyGovID** account to apply online
- your current or most recent foreign driving licence. You must hand it in, and applications without it are rejected.
- proof of residence in Ireland
- the fee of **€55**, paid by card

If your licence has expired, or a category on it has no start date, NDLS may ask for a **letter of entitlement** from the authority that issued it.

## Step 1: Check your eligibility

Find your country on the NDLS recognised states list. Note any limits, as some agreements only cover cars, not trucks or buses.

## Step 2: Apply

Apply online at ndls.ie with your MyGovID, or book an appointment at an NDLS centre.

## Step 3: Wait for your licence

In some cases NDLS must contact the country that issued your licence, which can add time.

## FAQ

**Can I exchange a US licence?**
Check the NDLS list. Ireland doesn't have a general agreement with the United States, so most US licences can't be exchanged.

**How long can I drive on a licence that can't be exchanged?**
A visitor can usually drive on a valid national licence for up to a year, but once you live here you need to start the Irish process.

**Do I lose my foreign licence?**
Yes. You hand it in when you exchange it.

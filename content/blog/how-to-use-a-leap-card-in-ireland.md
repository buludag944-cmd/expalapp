If you take buses, Luas or trains in Ireland, a **TFI Leap Card** will save you money compared with paying cash. It is a reusable prepaid travel card that works across the Transport for Ireland network. Here is how to get the most from it.

## What the Leap Card does

You top up the card with travel credit, then tap it when you travel. With a Leap Card you get:

- cheaper fares than cash
- the **TFI 90 Minute Fare**, which covers transfers
- **fare capping**, so you never pay more than a set amount per day or week

There are cards for adults, students, young adults and children. Student and young adult cards give lower fares if you are eligible.

## The TFI 90 Minute Fare in Dublin

In Dublin's Zone 1 you can travel for **90 minutes** across Dublin Bus, Go-Ahead Ireland city buses, Luas, and most DART and commuter rail services for one fare:

- **€2.00** adult
- **€1.00** young adult or student
- **€0.65** child

Express buses cost more. The fare is taken at your first tap, and later eligible journeys within 90 minutes are included, as long as you **tap on for each journey**.

## The TFI 90 Minute Fare in Cork

In Cork, a 90 Minute Fare applies on eligible TFI Cork City bus services. The adult fare is **€1.70**. It doesn't apply on rail or some routes, so check the TFI page.

## Fare caps in Dublin

In Zone 1, adult fares are capped at **€6.00 a day** and **€24.00 a week** (Monday to Sunday) across any mix of Dublin Bus, Go-Ahead Ireland, Luas, DART and Zone 1 commuter rail. Once you reach the cap, the rest of your travel on those services is free for that period.

## Tapping rules

1. On buses, tap the card on the reader each time you board.
2. On Luas, tap on at the platform validator **before** you board and tap off when you leave.
3. On DART and trains, tap on and off at the station.
4. If you pay for more than one person on one card, the 90 Minute Fare and caps won't apply.

Without a valid tap on Luas, you risk a standard fare notice of **€100**.

## FAQ

**Where can I get a Leap Card?**
At many shops and ticket machines, and online. You can top up in shops, at machines or in the TFI Leap app.

**Does the 90 Minute Fare work on commuter buses?**
Not on Dublin Commuter bus services. Check the TFI page for which services are included.

**Is a Leap Card cheaper than paying cash?**
Yes. Leap fares are lower than cash fares, and cash fares don't get capping.

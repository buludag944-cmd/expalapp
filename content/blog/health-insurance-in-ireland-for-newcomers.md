Ireland has a public health system, and private health insurance is **optional**. Many people buy it for faster access to some treatments and private hospital care. If you are thinking about it, the timing matters more than you might expect.

## Community rating

Irish health insurance is **community rated**. For a given plan, insurers charge everyone the same basic price, whatever their age, gender or health.

## Lifetime Community Rating (LCR)

There is one important exception. Under **Lifetime Community Rating**, people who first buy inpatient health insurance at age **35 or older** pay an extra charge called a **loading**.

- The loading is **2% of the gross premium for each year** you are over 34 when you first take out cover.
- The maximum loading is 70%.
- For example, someone who first buys cover at 37 pays a 6% loading (3 years × 2%).

Only inpatient health insurance counts. Health insurance **cash plans** don't reduce a loading.

## The nine-month rule for people moving to Ireland

If you are moving to Ireland, the Health Insurance Authority (HIA) explains that buying health insurance **within nine months** of becoming resident can help you avoid a loading. If you wait longer, a loading may apply depending on your age.

Previous periods of health insurance cover can also be taken into account when your insurer works out any loading. Have proof of earlier cover ready.

## Breaks in cover

You can have a break in cover of up to **13 weeks** without it affecting your LCR loading. Longer breaks can increase it.

## How to compare plans

1. Decide what matters to you, such as hospital cover, outpatient benefits or maternity cover.
2. Use the HIA's free comparison tool to compare plans from all registered insurers.
3. Check **waiting periods**, as new customers usually wait before they can claim for some treatments.
4. Ask insurers how they will treat your previous cover and your residence date.

## FAQ

**Is private health insurance required in Ireland?**
Not for most residents. Some immigration permissions, such as student permissions, may have their own insurance requirements, so check your permission.

**Does a loading apply if I'm under 35?**
No. Loadings only apply to people aged 35 and over who first buy cover late.

**Who regulates health insurance?**
The Health Insurance Authority, an independent statutory regulator.

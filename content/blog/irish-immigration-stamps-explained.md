When you register your immigration permission in Ireland, you receive a "stamp". The stamp is a short label for your type of permission. It tells employers, banks and government offices what you are allowed to do in Ireland, and it decides whether your time here counts towards citizenship. Here is what the main stamps mean.

## Why your stamp matters

Your stamp affects three practical things:

- whether you can work, and under what conditions
- how long you can stay before renewing
- whether your time counts as **reckonable residence** for citizenship

Always check the exact conditions on your own permission letter and IRP card. The summary below is a guide, not a substitute for your own documents.

## Stamp 1

Stamp 1 is the usual permission for people working in Ireland on an **employment permit**, such as a Critical Skills Employment Permit or a General Employment Permit. You can only work in the job your permit covers. Time on Stamp 1 is reckonable for citizenship.

## Stamp 1G

Stamp 1G covers two main groups:

- graduates of Irish higher education on the Third Level Graduate Programme, who can look for work and work without a permit for a limited period
- spouses and partners of Critical Skills Employment Permit holders, who can work without a separate permit

Time on Stamp 1G is reckonable.

## Stamp 2

Stamp 2 is for full-time non-EU students on an eligible course. Students can usually work up to **20 hours a week during term** and up to **40 hours a week in the holiday periods**. There is an overall time limit on how long you can stay as a student. Time on Stamp 2 is **not reckonable** for citizenship.

## Stamp 3

Stamp 3 is often given to dependent family members of some employment permit holders and to certain other groups. It **does not allow you to work**. Time on Stamp 3 is reckonable.

## Stamp 4

Stamp 4 is the stamp most people aim for. It lets you **work without an employment permit**, change employers freely and set up a business. Common routes include family members of Irish citizens and people moving up from Stamp 1. Time on Stamp 4 is reckonable.

## Stamp 5

Stamp 5 is granted to people who have lived in Ireland for a long time. It has no time limit and is valid until your passport expires.

## Moving from Stamp 1 to Stamp 4

You can apply to change from Stamp 1 to Stamp 4 online through the Immigration Service portal once you meet the conditions:

1. Critical Skills Employment Permit holders, researchers on a Hosting Agreement and some non-consultant hospital doctors can apply after **21 months of employment** in Ireland on that basis.
2. General Employment Permit and Intra-Company Transfer permit holders can apply after **57 months of employment** in Ireland.
3. You need proof of your employment, including Employment Detail Summaries from Revenue myAccount.

## FAQ

**Does time on a student stamp count towards citizenship?**
No. Time on Stamp 2 is not reckonable residence.

**Can I change jobs on Stamp 1?**
Only with a new or changed employment permit. On Stamp 4 you can change employers without a permit.

**Where do I apply to change stamps?**
Stamp changes are done online through the Immigration Service portal, not in person.

An Irish bank account makes almost everything easier. Your employer will want to pay your salary into it, your landlord may want rent by transfer, and many bills are paid by direct debit. Here is how to open one when you have just arrived.

## What banks must check

Before opening an account, Irish banks must confirm **who you are** and **where you live**. This is a legal anti-money-laundering requirement, so every bank asks for it, not just the strict ones.

You will usually need:

- **photo ID**, such as your passport or national ID card
- **proof of your address in Ireland**, such as a recent utility bill, a bank or credit card statement, or a letter from a government office

Proof of address documents generally need to be recent. Many banks accept documents dated within the last six months. Each bank sets its own list of accepted documents, so check its website before you go.

## No proof of address yet?

This is the most common problem for newcomers. You may have just signed a lease and have no bills in your name. Things that can help:

- ask the bank which letters it accepts from a new employer, college or landlord
- use official post addressed to you, such as letters from Revenue or the Department of Social Protection
- ask whether a digital or app-based bank accepts a different set of documents

If you open an account remotely or through an app, you may be asked for **more** documents, for example two proofs of identity and two proofs of address.

## Step 1: Compare accounts

Look at:

1. monthly or quarterly account fees
2. card and transaction charges
3. whether you can open the account online or need to visit a branch
4. how the bank handles international transfers, if you send money home

## Step 2: Gather your documents

Put your passport, your IRP card if you have one, and your proof of address together. Scan them too, as online applications often ask for photos.

## Step 3: Apply

Apply online, in the app or in a branch. Some banks verify you with a short video or selfie check.

## Step 4: Update your details

Once your account is open, give your IBAN to your employer and set up your rent and bill payments.

## FAQ

**Can I open an account before I have a PPS number?**
Some banks will open an account without a PPS number, but policies differ. Ask the bank directly.

**Do I need to visit a branch?**
Not always. Many banks let you apply online or in an app.

**Does a joint account need more documents?**
Yes. Each person on a joint account must prove their identity and address.

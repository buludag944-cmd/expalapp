Your Irish Residence Permit (IRP) card shows the date your permission expires. Renewing it is now an **online process** through the Immigration Service portal, wherever you live in Ireland. Here is how it works.

## When to apply

You can apply to renew **up to 12 weeks before** your IRP card expires. The Immigration Service strongly encourages people to apply at the 12-week point, because processing can take weeks.

Applying early doesn't cost you any time: your new permission runs from the expiry date of your current one.

Students on Stamp 2 must wait until their new course has started before they apply.

## Who can apply online

You must be **in Ireland** when you apply. Applications submitted from outside Ireland aren't accepted.

If you and a family member both need to renew, you must apply **separately**.

## Step 1: Create or log in to your account

Use the Immigration Service online portal. Create an account if you don't have one.

## Step 2: Prepare your documents

Check the required documents list for your stamp type. You will need digital copies, such as clear scans or photos. These usually include:

- the biometric page of your current passport
- your current IRP card
- documents for your permission type, such as proof of employment or college enrolment

## Step 3: Complete the form and pay

1. Select the registration renewal form.
2. Upload your documents.
3. Pay the fee by card, if it applies. The fee for most permissions is **€300**.
4. Submit and keep your application receipt and reference number.

## Step 4: Wait for your new card

Once processing is complete, your new card can take up to about **15 business days** to arrive by post. Make sure your address is correct.

## If your card expires while you wait

If you applied before your card expired, the Immigration Service has said you keep the conditions of your previous permission for **up to 12 weeks after expiry** while you wait for a decision. Keep your application receipt to show your employer. Travel rules can differ, so check the latest notices before you book trips.

## FAQ

**Do I need an appointment to renew?**
No. Renewals are online. You only attend in person if the Immigration Service asks you to.

**Can I change my stamp when I renew?**
Yes. Applications to change stamp category are made through the same online renewal system.

**What if my card expired a long time ago?**
Contact the Immigration Service. Long-expired permissions may need a different process.
